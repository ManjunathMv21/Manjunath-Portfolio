About Me

I'm Manjunath M V, a final-year Electronics & Communication Engineering student at Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering, Bengaluru. I like building things that sense, compute and respond — from ESP32 and Arduino firmware that talks to real sensors, to CMOS circuits in Cadence, to Verilog testbenches that prove a design works.

My work spans the whole stack: Embedded C and real-time data acquisition, analog and digital circuit design and testing, oscilloscope-and-multimeter debugging, and simulation in LTspice, Cadence Virtuoso, ModelSim, MATLAB and COMSOL.

I'm open to internships and entry-level roles in embedded, hardware, test and process engineering — and to collaborating on anything with a circuit board in it.
